The idea behind TEHCLAND

A place for nature.
A sense of belonging.

An Ontario project in development connecting native habitat restoration and outdoor learning with plans for a nature-based gathering space attentive to neurodivergent people’s needs.

Our story

An idea taking root.

TEHCLAND grew from a land-based idea in Ontario: care for native habitats while creating a place where people who feel overlooked in conventional settings could learn and spend time in nature.

Its environmental and educational aims belong together. Native planting, pollinator gardens, and waterway stewardship could become opportunities to learn practical ecological skills. The gathering space remains proposed, with sensory needs, quieter participation, and different ways of communicating central to its purpose.

Today, this website sets out the project’s direction. Retreats, learning facilities, and renewable energy installations remain future possibilities. Public visits are not currently offered through this website.

Our shared values

Looking ahead
