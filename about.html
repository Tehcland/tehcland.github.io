The idea behind TEHCLAND

A place for nature.
A sense of belonging.

An Ontario-based project vision connecting ecological restoration, education, and a more inclusive relationship with the natural world.

Our story

An idea taking root.

TEHCLAND’s public website began in August 2025 with a vision: bring care for ecosystems and care for people into the same conversation.

That vision includes native habitat restoration, opportunities to learn outdoors, and spaces that welcome neurodiverse people and others who may feel left out of conventional settings.

Today, this website sets out the project’s direction. Retreats, learning facilities, and renewable energy installations remain future possibilities. Public visits are not currently offered through this website.

Our shared values

Care is the common ground.

Ecology comes first.

Let habitat needs, local conditions, and long-term stewardship guide the choices we explore.

Different minds belong.

Consider neurodiversity, sensory needs, and accessible participation as the vision develops.

Grow with honesty.

Be clear about what is proposed, what needs investigation, and what has actually been achieved.

Looking ahead
