Areas of interest

01 / Ecology

Native habitats & pollinators

Ecological planning, appropriate native planting, and ideas for habitat restoration and waterway stewardship.

02 / Education

Learning in nature

Development of practical outdoor learning opportunities, restoration skills, and accessible educational resources.

03 / Inclusion

Community & accessibility

Planning for neurodiverse participation, sensory needs, and inclusive nature-based experiences.

04 / Exploration

Responsible energy research

Feasibility and environmental investigation of renewable micro-hydro, including the potential role of battery storage.
