New Edan · A vision for the future

Imagine a different
way to grow together.

TEHCLAND’s plans connect ecological restoration, practical outdoor learning, and an inclusive nature-based gathering space. They sit alongside the related New Edan vision; these ideas are not completed facilities or currently offered programmes.

The possibilities

A living vision,
grounded in care.

  1. 01

    Restore native habitats.

    Explore native planting, pollinator gardens, and waterway stewardship informed by ecological needs and local conditions.

  2. 02

    Learn through experience.

    Envision outdoor education, practical restoration skills, and opportunities to understand how healthy ecosystems work. No scheduled programmes are offered through this website.

  3. 03

    Create room to belong.

    Consider calm, inclusive ways to connect with nature, with neurodiverse participants and varied accessibility needs in mind. Retreat spaces remain a future possibility.

  4. 04

    Explore renewable micro-hydro.

    Investigate whether small-scale renewable generation could suit the project. Micro-hydro and battery storage are compatible; storage may help balance generation and demand.

    This is exploratory. No operating installation, generation capacity, or approved design is confirmed. Feasibility, water flows, habitat impacts, land-use requirements, and applicable permits would need assessment before any work.

How we hope to grow

Stewardship before
infrastructure.

Good intentions need careful groundwork. Our approach is to understand ecological conditions, seek appropriate expertise, consider accessibility, and investigate the requirements of any proposed activity.

A clear distinction

Ideas on this page are not claims of completed construction, secured permits, established land trusts, or operating facilities. Partnerships and funding would support investigation and development, rather than certify these outcomes.
